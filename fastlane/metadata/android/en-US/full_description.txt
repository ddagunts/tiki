tiki is a Tesla key for Android. It talks the public Tesla BLE key protocol over Bluetooth Low Energy — no Internet permission, no Google services, no Tesla cloud. The only runtime permission it asks for is <b>Nearby devices</b>. The pairing keypair is generated and stored inside your phone's secure element; the private half never leaves it.

<b>Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.</b> "Tesla" is a trademark of Tesla, Inc. This is independent software that speaks the published BLE key protocol. It is not a replacement for the official Tesla app and is not intended to be relied on as your sole means of entering or operating your car.

<b>What it does</b>

Once paired with the car, tiki can:

* Lock and unlock the doors
* Auto-unlock on approach and auto-lock on departure with opt-in per-car Proximity unlock (beta)
* Open and close the trunk and charge port; vent and close the windows
* Climate: toggle climate, set driver / passenger target temperatures (with 68 / 72 °F presets), bioweapon-defense mode, steering-wheel heat, precondition max
* Audio / media: play / pause, next / previous track, next / previous favorite, volume up / down
* Seats: heat and cooler levels for every seat; auto-seat climate
* Climate keeper: Off / On / Dog / Camp modes, plus cabin overheat protection
* Charging: start / stop, charge-limit presets, charging-current limit
* Sentry mode on/off, flash lights, honk horn
* Refresh and display vehicle status (lock state, sleep state, user presence, closures including frunk and tonneau, range, battery percent)
* Auto-reconnect with backoff when the BLE link drops

<b>What it isn't</b>

* Not a replacement for the official Tesla app. Anything that needs Tesla's cloud is out of scope by design.
* Not a long-range key. BLE range is roughly walking-up-to-the-car distance.
* Not a UWB phone-as-key. Proximity unlock uses BLE RSSI, calibrated per car via live readout in Settings.

<b>How it works under the hood</b>

* BLE discovery: matches the Tesla-broadcast T&lt;8-byte-VIN-hash&gt; short-name beacon for your VIN
* GATT connect, MTU negotiation, notifications enabled on the vehicle's RX / TX characteristics
* ECDH (P-256) with the vehicle's static public key inside Android Keystore
* AES-128-GCM session keyed per the Tesla reference implementation
* VCSEC command / response framing with replay-protection counters

<b>Permissions</b>

* <b>Nearby devices</b> (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — required to discover and talk to the car
* <b>Foreground service (connectedDevice)</b> — keeps the BLE session alive for Proximity unlock
* <b>Notifications</b> — for the foreground-service notification (Android 13+)

No INTERNET permission. No location permission (scan uses <i>neverForLocation</i>). No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
