Fix first-time setup:

- Auto-reconnect no longer interrupts initial enrollment. While a car is unpaired, a dropped BLE link parks the controller in a Disconnected state instead of looping. A new Reconnect card lets you retry by hand, then press Enroll and tap your keycard without the link being yanked.
- After the first successful enrollment (or a session that comes back already on the car's whitelist), normal auto-reconnect resumes.
